📜 Know Your Constitutional Rights

Fundamental Rights guaranteed by the Constitution of India.

Art. 14

Right to Equality

Equality before law and equal protection of laws for all citizens.

Art. 19

Right to Freedom

Freedom of speech, expression, movement, and to practice any profession.

Art. 21

Right to Life

No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law.

Art. 21A

Right to Education

The state shall provide free and compulsory education to children 6-14 years of age.

Art. 22

Protection in Arrest

Right to be informed of grounds of arrest and consult a lawyer when arrested.

Art. 25

Freedom of Religion

Freedom of conscience and free profession, practice, and propagation of religion.

Art. 32

Right to Remedies

Right to move Supreme Court for enforcement of fundamental rights.

Art. 15

No Discrimination

No discrimination on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex, or place of birth.

📖 Important IPC / BNS Sections

Common criminal law sections everyone should know. Understand what offense has been committed.

IPC 302

Punishment for Murder

Whoever commits murder shall be punished with death or imprisonment for life, and shall also be liable to fine.

IPC 354

Assault on Women

Assault or use of criminal force on a woman with intent to outrage her modesty — up to 2 years imprisonment or fine or both.

IPC 376

Punishment for Rape

Sexual assault is a serious offense with severe punishment including rigorous imprisonment of at least 10 years.

IPC 420

Cheating & Fraud

Dishonestly inducing delivery of property through cheating — up to 7 years imprisonment and fine.

IPC 323

Voluntarily Causing Hurt

Voluntarily causing hurt — imprisonment up to 1 year or fine up to ₹1,000 or both.

IPC 506

Criminal Intimidation / Threatening

Threatening another with injury to person/reputation/mental harm — imprisonment up to 2 years or fine.

IPC 509

Word, Gesture Intended to Insult Modesty

Uttering words or gestures intending to insult the modesty of a woman — imprisonment up to 1 year or fine or both.

IPC 379

Punishment for Theft

Whoever commits theft shall be punished with imprisonment up to 3 years or fine or both.

IPC 306

Abetment of Suicide

If any person commits suicide, whoever abets shall be punished with imprisonment up to 10 years and fine.

IPC 498A

Domestic Violence by Husband

Cruelty by husband or relatives of husband — imprisonment up to 3 years and fine. Women can also file under DV Act 2005.

IPC 384

Extortion (Blackmail)

Putting a person in fear of injury and dishonestly inducing them to deliver property — imprisonment up to 3 years.

IPC 441

Criminal Trespass & Stalking

Entering another's property with intent to commit an offense or to annoy — imprisonment up to 3 months or fine.

🛡️ Free Legal Aid & Rights

Every citizen has the right to free legal assistance. Know your protections.

👨‍⚖️

Consult a Government Lawyer

If you can't afford a lawyer, you have the right to free legal aid under Article 39A. Visit your nearest Legal Services Authority (DLSA).

📝

File an FIR

Every citizen has the right to file a First Information Report (FIR) at the police station. If the police refuse, write to the SP or approach court.

📱

Cyber Crime Portal

For online fraud, cheating, or cybercrime, report at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.

⚖️

One-Nation One-Number

Dial 112 for all emergencies — police, ambulance, fire, and disaster response from anywhere in India.
